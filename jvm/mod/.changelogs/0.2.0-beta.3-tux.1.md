Our server's first build of GTNH Planner, on top of beta 3. GTNH 2.9 only. Back up your world before updating.

Most of this release is under the hood: the planner's maths, layout and saving were rebuilt on shared code. Numbers match the game more closely, and a plan looks the same wherever you open it.

## New

- Machine numbers follow the pack's own rates: recipe speed, bee yield and crop yield.
- The Overview has an Amps setting: each machine's power reads in amps of its own tier.
- The Multi Smelter has a coil setting. Each coil tier doubles its parallels.
- The Industrial Sledgehammer and the Large Thermal Refinery have a solenoid setting.
- The Component Assembly Line takes its hatch amps and a casing setting.
- The Solar Boiler and the Solar Tower have a Duty setting, like the solar panels.
- Bee cards can use a plain Apiary or a Bee House. The Alveary and the Mega Apiary come in from plan links as themselves.
- The Algae Farm uses power and grows the recipe of its hatch's tier, one tier higher with compost.

## Better

- Big multiblocks stop at the parallels their power can pay for.
- Steam multiblocks round each run up to a whole tick. Steam machines aren't offered for recipes over 32 EU/t or for fluid recipes they can't take.
- Single block machines only make the outputs they have slots for. The Steam Grinder and the Windmill make only their first output.
- Furnaces, the Multi Smelter and the Steam Hearth smelt at the pack's speed and show their power.
- The IsaMill and the Flotation Cell overclock perfectly. The L.A.T.E.X., the 3D Copying Machine and the Spinmatron run on their own rules.
- The Rock Breaker and the Scanner show their real run times. Overclocks follow the game for coil heat, the Pyrolyse Oven, the Oil Cracker and the Large Chemical Reactor.
- Power cards follow the game: the plasma turbine fuel list, boiler fuels and burn times, fusion, the HTGR, the heat exchangers, the rocket and combustion engines. A generator whose fuel won't fit its buffer says so.
- Plans with huge EU next to small item rates now solve.
- Numbers read the same everywhere. Tiny rates keep their decimals, huge ones go up to E, and a count of 100 no longer shows as 101.
- Every count shows its rule, Any included, with a Set box beside it.
- Wires and Arrange use a new router and layout. Wires may take different paths, but your cards stay where you put them.
- The planner only talks to the community site our server sets up, and to nothing at all when none is set.

## Fixed

- A slot only takes what its recipe lists. Lapis Lazuli no longer goes into a Lapis Dust slot.
- Fluid tanks follow the Canner, so molten platinum group metal cells fill instead of emptying.
- A count of 0.5 stays 0.5.
- Plans you sync, share or export keep their drawer wires connected, and a shared machine keeps all its recipes.
- The update offer knows our server's own releases.

## Install

Put `gtnhplanner-0.2.0-beta.3-tux.1.jar` in your `mods` folder and delete the old one. Your plans from beta 3 open as they were.

Our server's second build of GTNH Planner, on top of beta 3. GTNH 2.9 only. Back up your world before updating.

This release brings website plans in whole, and lets the planner reach a site behind an access header.

## New

- A website plan opens with every card on the board. Cards the game cannot run now show at their place, with the plan's name, ports, counts, wires and drawers. These are cards switched off on the website, ore veins, small ores, underground fluids, Thaumcraft and essentia recipes, recipes this pack lacks, and cards with no recipe in the plan. Before, they were left off the board.
- Those cards say VIEW ONLY on their foot, with the reason. You can move, wire and delete them. Their settings, count and recipe can only be changed on the website.
- A view-only card counts in the solve when the website's numbers cover its recipe (ore veins, underground fluids, a recipe this pack lacks), at the plan's count. A switched-off card, a card with no recipe, and bee or crop cards stay outside the solve, and their wires carry nothing.
- A generator card shows a reading the website pinned to its strip when the plan comes through cloud sync. A plan opened from a file or the library keeps those pins for the website but does not show them yet.

## Better

- Exporting, posting or syncing such a plan writes those cards back as the website made them, with the drawers wired to them and their wires. A view-only card you move goes out at its new place. One you delete is gone, with its recipe and wires.
- With cloud plans on, moving or deleting a view-only card reaches the cloud. A card deleted on the website leaves your board with its wires.
- Saves from the previous build kept these cards out of sight. They now show when the plan is opened.
- The import report lists these cards as shown, not as left out.
- The custom rate card and the fluid tank read like the other cards. The custom rate's count line shows its rule, and its rate reads and types in the board's rate unit (per second, minute or hour).
- Generator cards read like the other cards. Number settings read "Fuel rate 1250 L/s". A pinned setting that another setting turns off stays on the card, greyed.
- A wrong password at sign-in shows the site's own message.

## Fixed

- A website drawer that is both fed and drawn from lost its drain setting (product, byproduct or trash) when the plan went back out. It now keeps it.
- A cloud plan lost the website's setting pins for machines the board has no card of when a later save changed the board's pins. They now stay.
- A sign-in the live service refused was tried again and again. It is now tried once, and your plans stay on this computer.
- A site that sends the game to a sign-in page now gets a plain message ("The site asked for a sign-in the mod cannot do") in the library, at sign-in and for cloud plans, instead of a read error.

## Install

Put `gtnhplanner-0.2.0-beta.3-tux.2.jar` in your `mods` folder and delete the old one. Your plans from beta 3 and from tux.1 open as they were.

If our server uses an access header, its admin gives you two lines for `config/gtnhplanner.cfg`. Without them, the library, sign-in and cloud plans say the site asked for a sign-in the mod cannot do.

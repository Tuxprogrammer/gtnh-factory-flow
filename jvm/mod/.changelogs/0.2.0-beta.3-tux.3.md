Our server's third build of GTNH Planner, on top of beta 3. GTNH 2.9 only. Back up your world before updating.

This release lets a card take another recipe or swap its own, and makes the number pad work in the planner.

## New

- A card's menu has **Add another recipe**: NEI opens on the card's machine, and the recipe you add with + becomes one more section of the card, on a machine that runs them all. The plan goes to the website as a shared machine, the same as one made there.
- A card's menu has **Replace the recipe**: NEI opens on the recipes that make what the card makes, and the recipe you add with + takes the card's place. It keeps its count rule and the wires the new recipe still has. When none of its wires would fit, the new card lands beside it instead. Close the NEI page without adding anything and + adds a new card again, as before.

## Better

- Creating an account from the game on a site that sends the game to a sign-in page now says "Create your account on the website, then sign in here." The sign-in and feedback boxes also wrap a long refusal onto a second line instead of cutting it off.

## Fixed

- The number pad now types into the planner's number boxes (counts, rates, amps, settings), notes and the sign-in password box: its digits, point and the plus, minus, times and divide keys. Before, the game gave those keys no character and nothing was typed.
- A website plan opened from a file, a paste, Open in the mod or a library download showed none of the settings and readings the website pinned to its cards, machines and generators alike. It now shows the same pins as the same plan through cloud sync, and writes them back as you leave them. An empty custom rate card or fluid tank in such a plan now comes back as the empty card it was, not as a view-only card.
- An Industrial Farm or Crop Manager card now rates its crops by the exact harvest time, not one rounded to whole ticks, so its output per second matches the website (for example 200.27 a second where the game showed 199.87). Its tooltip shows the same time to the hundredth.
- Rounded figures such as amps and machine counts that sit exactly on a half now print as they should.
- Very large EU/t totals on the Industrial Coke Oven, Mega Oil Cracker, Mega Alloy Blast Smelter, Large Fluid Extractor and Large Thermal Refinery now match the game to the last unit.

## Install

Put `gtnhplanner-0.2.0-beta.3-tux.3.jar` in your `mods` folder and delete the old one. The same jar goes on the server and on every client, updated together. Your plans from tux.2 open as they were.

Nothing changes in `config/gtnhplanner.cfg`.

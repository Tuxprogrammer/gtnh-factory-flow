## New

- `/gtnhplanner bookmark <id>` adds a shared plan's inputs and outputs to your NEI bookmarks.

## Better

- Void Miner cards draw their real power and run their 10-tick cycle.
- Chemical Plant, Large Thermal Refinery and Large Fluid Extractor cards use the coil rules the game states now.
- After adding the assistant's change, the board shows the new cards.

## Fixed

- Plans saved before an item was renamed open with those cards running.
- Live plans with renamed items no longer rebuild those cards on every edit someone makes.
- Adding the assistant's change keeps wires from new cards to your existing drawers.
- Plan files downloaded from the library open in the mod.
- A forced leftover no longer lists a drawer's port as a place it could go.
- A leftover split you set survives combining twin cards.

## New

- Pattern the plan makes as many patterns as fit your inventory. Run it again for the next ones until the plan is done.
- Deleting more than one card asks first.

## Better

- Boxes never run off a short screen: the pattern box, Settings, a card's settings and the spare box scroll with their keys in view, and long menus have a scroll bar you can drag.
- Combine gives the same machine whichever card sits top-left: the highest tier, supply, coil and settings any card had.
- A plan saved in a world before an item was renamed in a pack update opens with its recipes, wires and drawers.
- Opening the same plan twice gives the same default place for a leftover.
- A careful click that moves a pixel or two still opens what you clicked.

## Fixed

- Ctrl+Z undoes and Ctrl+Shift+Z or Ctrl+Y redo on the board, once per press.
- Scroll bars and sliders follow the mouse, and a click on the board no longer locks the overview's bar.
- Pattern the plan no longer loses patterns when your inventory is full.
- The inventory stays up to date while the planner is open and after it closes.
- A cut or copied shared machine pastes as one shared machine, with its recipes, count and byproduct drawers.
- Magnetic Flux Exhibitor, Industrial Cutting Factory, Electric Implosion Compressor and both Precise Assembler modes show each setting once.
- While a tour, update or crash offer is showing, keys and clicks do not change your plan underneath it.

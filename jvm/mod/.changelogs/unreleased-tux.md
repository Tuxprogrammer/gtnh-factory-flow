## New

- Pattern this, in a recipe card's menu, turns a blank pattern from your inventory into an AE2 pattern of the card's recipe (one for each recipe of a shared machine): the same pattern as moving the recipe from NEI into a pattern terminal and pressing Encode. It needs AE2 and Not Enough Energistics, and this mod on the server too.

## Fixed

- Very short recipes in multiblocks run as many parallels as the game gives them. When a recipe takes half a tick or less after the machine's speed bonus or heat overclocks (a 1-tick recipe in a Large Electric Compressor, a Black Hole Compressor or most GT++ multiblocks), the game multiplies the parallels again; cards now show that rate (usually 2x, more on blast furnaces and dehydrators, up to what the hatch can pay for), so fewer machines are needed.
- The overview's scroll bar can be dragged again. A press on the thumb or just beside it no longer grabs the overview's width handle, which held the list still; the handle now sits wholly on the board side of the edge.

## New

- A card's menu has Add another recipe: NEI opens on the card's machine, and the recipe you add with + becomes one more section of the card, on a machine that runs them all. The plan goes to the website as a shared machine, the same as one made there.
- A card's menu has Replace the recipe: NEI opens on the recipes that make what the card makes, and the recipe you add with + takes the card's place, keeping its count rule and the wires the new recipe still has. When none of its wires would fit, the new card lands beside it instead.
- Close the NEI page without adding anything and + adds a new card again, as before.

## Better

- Creating an account from the game on a site that sends the game to a sign-in page now says "Create your account on the website, then sign in here." The sign-in and feedback boxes also wrap a long refusal onto a second line instead of cutting it off.

## Fixed

- A website plan opened from a file, a paste, Open in the mod or a library download showed none of the settings and readings the website pinned to its cards, machines and generators alike. It now shows the same pins as the same plan through cloud sync, and writes them back as you leave them.
- An empty custom rate card or an empty fluid tank came back from a plan file as a view-only card. It now comes back as the empty card it was, at its rate and direction.
